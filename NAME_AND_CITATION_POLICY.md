# RELXILL Name and Citation Policy

## 1. Official Version of RELXILL

“RELXILL” refers to the official X-ray relativistic reflection model developed and maintained by Thomas Dauser, Javier Garica, and collaborators.

The authoritative version of RELXILL is the version distributed through the official webpage at www.sternwarte.uni-erlangen.de/research/relxill/.

Modified or derived versions of the code must clearly indicate that they are modified and must not be distributed under the name “RELXILL” without explicit permission from the original authors.

This policy is intended to preserve scientific clarity and avoid confusion in the literature.


## 2. Scientific Citation

If you use RELXILL in scientific publications, presentations, or other scholarly work, please cite these two references:

- Garcia et al., 2014, ApJ, 782, 76
- Dauser et al., 2014, MNRAS, 444, L100

If specific extensions or variants of RELXILL are used, please cite the corresponding publications describing those versions.

Proper citation ensures that the scientific development of the model is acknowledged and supports continued maintenance and development.


## 3. Redistribution and Integration

RELXILL is distributed under the terms of the LICENSE file included in this repository.

If RELXILL is redistributed, integrated into other software packages, or modified:

- The original copyright notices must be preserved.
- The license terms must be respected.
- Modifications should be clearly documented.

Derived works must not imply endorsement by the original authors unless explicit permission has been granted.

## 4. Community and Good Scientific Practice

RELXILL is a scientific research tool. Its continued development relies on:

- Proper attribution
- Transparent reporting of modifications
- Clear version identification in publications

When publishing results obtained with RELXILL, please indicate the version number used.
